🔧 Tubal Factor
  • Mild tubal disease: Consider tubal surgery or proceed to IVF
  • Bilateral tubal blockage: IVF is treatment of choice
  • Hydrosalpinx: Salpingectomy before IVF (improves success rates)
  • Proximal tubal obstruction: Consider tubal cannulation
🎯 Endometriosis
  • Minimal-mild: Laparoscopic ablation may improve pregnancy rates
  • Moderate-severe: Consider surgery before IVF or proceed directly to IVF
  • Endometrioma: Cystectomy if >4cm before IVF (balance against ovarian reserve)
  • Medical suppression (GnRH agonist) NOT recommended for fertility
🔬 Diminished Ovarian Reserve
  • Counselling: Time-sensitive - earlier treatment recommended
  • IVF: May require higher gonadotropin doses
  • Consider: Donor oocytes if very low reserve or poor response
  • DHEA/CoQ10: Limited evidence but sometimes used empirically
📋 Unexplained Infertility Pathway

NICE Recommendation:

  1. Expectant management 12 months if woman <36 years (cumulative pregnancy rate 50%)
  2. IUI (unstimulated or stimulated) - limited evidence but may offer 3-6 cycles
  3. IVF if no conception after 2 years or woman ≥36 years

Female Lifestyle Recommendations

✅ Recommendations
  • BMI 19-25: Optimal for conception
  • Folic acid: Start 3 months before conception
  • Regular intercourse: Every 2-3 days throughout cycle
  • Moderate exercise: 150 min/week
  • Cervical screening: Up to date
  • Rubella immunity: Check and vaccinate if needed
  • Reduce caffeine: <200mg/day
❌ Avoid
  • Smoking: Reduces fertility by 50%, increases miscarriage
  • Alcohol: Avoid completely when trying to conceive
  • Recreational drugs: All harmful to fertility
  • Extreme exercise: Can cause anovulation
  • Underweight (BMI <19): Causes anovulation
  • Obesity (BMI >30): Reduces fertility, IVF success
  • NSAIDs: May inhibit ovulation

Dietary Recommendations

✅ INCLUDE
  • Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel)
  • Nuts & seeds (walnuts, pumpkin)
  • Leafy greens (spinach, kale)
  • Tomatoes (lycopene)
  • Berries (antioxidants)
  • Eggs (vitamin D, choline)
  • Lean meats (zinc, carnitine)
  • Oysters (zinc)
❌ AVOID/LIMIT
  • Processed meats
  • Trans fats
  • High-sugar foods
  • Excessive soy
  • Alcohol (<14 units/week)
  • Caffeine (<300mg/day)
  • BPA-containing packaging

Lifestyle (EAU Grade A)

EAU 2024: "Inform infertile men about detrimental effects of obesity, low physical activity, smoking, and high alcohol intake on sperm quality."

1

BMI 18.5-24.9

Obesity reduces sperm quality. Target 5-10% weight loss if overweight.

2

Stop Smoking

Reduces concentration 13-17%, motility 10%, increases DNA fragmentation.

3

Exercise 150 min/week

Moderate activity improves testosterone. Avoid excessive endurance training.

4

Reduce Scrotal Heat

Avoid hot baths, saunas, laptops on lap. Wear loose boxer shorts.

5

Sleep 7-9 hours

Chronic stress/poor sleep affects hormonal balance.
